# Frequently asked customer questions

## How do I ask for a recommendation?

Describe what you are looking for, for example a category, a price range, or a number of people. The assistant searches the live menu and shows matching dishes. It can only use menu data that the restaurant actually stores.

## Can the assistant tell me whether a dish is vegetarian or contains an allergen?

No. The menu database currently has no dietary or allergen fields. The assistant will not guess from a dish name. Please confirm dietary and allergen questions with restaurant staff before ordering.

## Can I order through the chat?

You can ask the assistant to prepare an order draft. Review the items, quantities, prices, total, and table number. The order is created only after you press the confirmation control and the backend revalidates everything.

## Can I reserve a table through the chat?

Yes, for a signed-in customer. Provide the party size, date, and time. The assistant checks stored availability and prepares a draft. The reservation is created only after you review and confirm it. Availability is not a guarantee until the backend accepts the reservation.

## Why do you need my phone number?

The reservation record includes a contact phone for the restaurant. If your account has no phone number, the assistant will ask for one before preparing the reservation draft.

## What if the assistant does not know the answer?

It should say that the information is not available in the restaurant system. It will not invent opening hours, ingredients, allergen information, or policies.
